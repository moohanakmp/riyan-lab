🌊 Flow — Recurring Time-of-Day Bias
How Flow analysis works
Looks for times of day where an asset has moved the same direction consistently across many days (not just once) — e.g. "gold tends bearish after 11pm." Pick a candle timeframe above: finer timeframes (1m/5m) reach fewer days back but with more precision; "1 Day" instead checks which weekdays trend consistently (a daily candle has no time-of-day left to analyze); "Any" cross-checks 15m/30m/1h and only keeps patterns confirmed by more than one. Only surfaces a pattern once it clears a real consistency bar, not any 50/50 coinflip. First run at a given timeframe fetches history and can take a little while.

🔎 Research
Research methodology & limits
Answers "has this happened before, and what happened after?" using real multi-year daily price history — finds historical days with a genuinely similar trend/RSI/volatility condition to today, then reports what actually happened over the following 5/10/20 days, as probabilities, never a guaranteed direction. Scope, honestly: this works at daily resolution (multi-year 15-minute history isn't fetchable from free rate-limited sources), and does not include historical news-event comparison — there's no free source of historical economic-calendar data with real forecast/actual/previous values to compare against, so that part of a full research spec isn't something this can build without a paid data source.
Tap 🔎 Research to pull multi-year daily history and search for similar historical setups.

🎯 Signal Logic (Trader's Rules)
6 checks, all weighted:
1. HTF Trend (EMA50) — never trade counter-trend
2. EMA9 vs EMA21 cross — short-term direction
3. RSI band avoiding overbought/oversold entries — tuned per asset (gold 45–62/38–55, silver 42–64/36–58, BTC 45–62/38–55, since silver's RSI swings more before a genuine trend confirms)
4. Ichimoku TK cross — trend momentum
5. Last candle body ≥40% — no dojis/spinning tops
6. Near S&R level ±0.5% — only enter at key price zones
Volatility gate: No signal if ATR too high or too low.
Reversal watch (1m/5m/15m/1h/4h): Every open trade is scanned across 5 timeframes — the same top-down stack pro traders use (4h/1h for macro bias, 15m for structure, 1m/5m for the earliest tell). 👀 WATCH = fast (1m/5m) momentum turns against you, structure still holds. ⚠️ FLIP = the 15m structural trend (same EMA50 used for entries) turns against you. 🔴 STRONG = 15m + 1h + 4h all against you — full confluence, exit territory. Levels always stay locked, but you can tap 🚪 Close Trade Now on any open trade, any time — not only on a warning.
🧠 Engine v3 (auto + manual): 12 safety gates (HTF agreement, volatility band, ADX trend strength, no-chase, spike candle, RSI exhaustion, news blackout, rollover hours, opposing volume, value zone, trigger candle, 15m structure) then a 0–100 quality score (4H/1H trend, structure, pullback location, trigger, momentum, volume, liquidity sweep). Fixes found in the v2 audit: SELL signals used to skip the 1H/4H check; "near support" passed when no support existed; three correlated momentum checks triple-counted one move; the strong-candle trigger chased moves.
📊 Volume Pressure: buy vs sell volume over the last 20 × 15m candles (estimated from where each candle closes in its range), cumulative delta, OBV, and divergence warnings. BTC uses Coinbase BTC/USD traded volume. Metals retain their live perpetual/token source when reachable; otherwise delayed COMEX futures history supplies reference volume. Futures contract volume is not spot/broker volume. The reference fallback supports analysis and replay only, with entries paused.
🧭 Manual & 📉 Analytics: always give a full plan — entry (market or limit at the pullback), stop beyond structure, TP1 1R / TP2 next liquidity / TP3 runner, $ risk at your lot size, and the exact price that proves the idea wrong. Graded CONFIRMED only when every gate passes; otherwise LOWER CONFIDENCE with what to wait for. Never counted in Dashboard or P&L.
🧪 Backtest: Analytics → Run backtest replays Engine v3 on the real candles on your device, with no look-ahead, and reports win rate, R and profit factor.
🔥 Spike badge: shown on the trend strip when ATR expands sharply vs its own recent average (or, for BTC, when real candle volume runs high) — a heads-up that a bigger move may be underway. Informational only — it doesn't change entries or exits.
Duplicate alerts: if KMR is open in more than one tab on the same device/browser at once, only one tab sends each Telegram/email alert — the rest detect they lost the claim and stay silent. A server lease selects one active paper engine per account across browsers; durable signal keys reject duplicate entries. The engine pauses while ownership or saved records are being restored.
🔒 Stability: All checks run on closed candles only — the live forming candle is ignored, so indicators can't flicker mid-candle. Once a signal fires, entry/TP1/TP2/TP3/SL are frozen until the trade closes, followed by a 15-min cooldown per symbol.
